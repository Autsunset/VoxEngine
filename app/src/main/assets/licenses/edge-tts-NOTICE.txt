Edge TTS v2 token generation

EdgeGecToken.kt is a Kotlin adaptation of src/edge_tts/drm.py from edge-tts:
https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts

The adaptation replaces Python's arbitrary-precision arithmetic with
32-bit wrapping arithmetic and uses a conventional SHA-256 round loop
instead of the upstream unrolled implementation.

app/src/main/java/com/voxengine/engine/edge/EdgeGecToken.kt
